# Aria — Pricing

## Included Support (All Meridian Plans)
Aria is included at no additional cost with every Meridian subscription.
There is no per-message fee, no per-ticket fee, and no usage cap for
standard support interactions.

| Meridian Plan | Monthly Price | Aria Access | Priority |
|--------------|--------------|-------------|----------|
| Starter | $49/user/mo | In-app chat, email | Standard (< 60s response) |
| Professional | $99/user/mo | Chat, email, Slack Connect | Priority (< 30s response) |
| Enterprise | Custom | All channels + dedicated instance | Premium (< 15s response, custom SLA) |

## What is Included
- Unlimited support conversations
- 24/7/365 availability
- Multi-language support (EN, ES, FR, DE, PT)
- Automatic ticket creation and routing
- Full conversation history and analytics

## What is Not Included
- Custom model fine-tuning (quoted separately)
- Languages beyond the standard five (quoted per language)
- On-premise deployment (Enterprise plan only, custom pricing)
- Compliance certifications for white-label (SOC 2, HIPAA — quoted separately)

## Billing
- Invoiced monthly on the 1st
- Net 30 payment terms for Enterprise
- Credit card or ACH for Starter and Professional
- Annual prepay discount: 15% off monthly rates
